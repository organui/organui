`acl-rupture` shows the unhappy triad on the knees of the whole-body joints model: the torn anterior cruciate ligament, medial collateral ligament and medial meniscus, with the haemarthrosis that follows. Its stages run from the acute injury to reconstruction with a graft, or to an untreated, unstable knee heading for osteoarthritis, with the Lachman and pivot shift tests and imaging at each. The rest of the body's joints fold away in the panel.
