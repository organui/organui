`acute-angle-closure-glaucoma` shows the emergency on the eye: the forward lens and iris closing the drainage angle, the shallow anterior chamber and the waterlogged cornea, with the optic nerve under threat. Its stages run from the attack to laser iridotomy, or to a cupped, damaged nerve when treatment comes late, with eye pressure, acuity, gonioscopy and the visual field at each.
