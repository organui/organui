Vessels divide the descending aorta at the diaphragm into `thoracic-aorta` and `abdominal-aorta`, and the pancreas divides at its neck into `pancreatic-head` and `pancreatic-body-and-tail`, so the abdominal aortic aneurysm and pancreatic cancer light up only the part they affect. The `descending-aorta` and `pancreas` structure ids are gone: rename them in your own findings, selections and visibility maps.
