A clinical review brings the fifteen conditions in line with current guidelines. Ischaemic stroke starts its DOAC within days, not months, and its aphasia is global; pituitary apoplexy puts the abducens nerve inside the cavernous sinus, not in its wall; an ectopic pregnancy is diagnosed by an adnexal mass on the scan, with a pregnancy of unknown location when nothing is seen. Treatments, timings and results are updated across the rest, such as PCI within 90 minutes and an SGLT2 inhibitor after myocardial infarction, immunotherapy first in BRAF-mutated melanoma, and azathioprine during induction in autoimmune hepatitis.
