`gallstone-disease` shows gallstones on the biliary tree, where the place a stone lodges decides the illness: biliary colic in the cystic duct, cholecystitis with Murphy's sign, cholangitis when the common duct blocks and the liver backs up, and pancreatitis at the ampulla. Each stage has the bloods, cultures and imaging to expect.
