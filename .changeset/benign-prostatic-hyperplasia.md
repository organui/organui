`benign-prostatic-hyperplasia` shows an enlarging prostate on two models: the prostate, where it narrows the urethra, and the bladder, which thickens to push past it. Its stages run from troublesome symptoms to chronic retention backing up into both ureters, or acute retention, with the IPSS, flow rate, residual volume, PSA and creatinine at each.
