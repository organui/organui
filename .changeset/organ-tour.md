`organ-tour`, a guided walk through organ models for explaining a condition to a patient. Each chapter shows a model at its real scale, from an organ down to a cell, with captions that highlight one structure at a time and move at the reader's pace. A question closes the chapter, and the summary names the chapters to go over again. It takes any organ model, and `conditionModel` for a condition's colours.
