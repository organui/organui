`laryngeal-cancer` shows a squamous cell carcinoma of the left vocal cord on the larynx: early disease causing hoarseness, a fixed cord once the muscle is invaded, and spread through the thyroid cartilage. Nasendoscopy, radiotherapy or laser, chemoradiotherapy and total laryngectomy sit on its timeline. The larynx gains a "Vocal cords" view, looking straight down on the glottis, that the disease opens on.
