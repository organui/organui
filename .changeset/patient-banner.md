`patient-banner` shows who a clinical screen is about, laid out as the NHS patient banner sets it: the family name in capitals, date of birth as "12-Jan-1958" with the age in days, months or years, sex, labelled identifiers, alerts, and allergies that read differently when not recorded, none known or recorded. `formatNhsNumber` and `isValidNhsNumber` space an NHS number 3-3-4 and check its modulus 11 digit.
