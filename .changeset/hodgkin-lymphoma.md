`hodgkin-lymphoma` shows nodular sclerosis Hodgkin lymphoma on a lymph node: collagen bands from a thickened capsule, effaced follicles, and Reed–Sternberg cells in the paracortex, with the lymphatics that carry it to the next node group. Its stages are active disease and remission after treatment, with biopsy, PET-CT and bloods at each.
