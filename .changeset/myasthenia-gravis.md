`myasthenia-gravis` shows antibody-positive myasthenia gravis on the eye and the thymus: fatigable ptosis and double vision with the pupils spared, and a thymoma in the left lobe. Its course runs from the eyes to generalised weakness or, as the alternative, a myasthenic crisis, with antibody testing, pyridostigmine, immunosuppression, thymectomy and intensive care on the timeline.
