`rheumatoid-arthritis` shows seropositive rheumatoid arthritis on the joints: symmetrical synovitis of the MCP, PIP, wrist and MTP joints, with the DIP joints spared. Its course branches from early disease into remission when treated to target, or erosions and deformity when it is not, with referral, methotrexate, treat to target and biologics on the timeline.
