The first disease component: `autoimmune-hepatitis` shows the condition on a liver lobule. Its structures are grouped as affected (the portal infiltrate, interface hepatitis at the limiting plate and the periportal hepatocytes) or usually spared (the other zones, the central vein and the bile ducts), and each one describes what the disease does there. It installs the liver lobule and the organ viewer.

`liver-lobule` is a new component on the organ viewer, from an original CC0 schematic model: the central vein, hepatocyte plates in their three zones, the limiting plate, sinusoids with Kupffer cells, and a portal tract with its venule, arteriole and bile duct at each corner.

The docs list diseases in their own sidebar group and in a diseases gallery grouped by body system.
