The first disease component: `autoimmune-hepatitis` shows the condition on the liver model. Its structures are grouped as affected (interface hepatitis in the parenchyma and portal tracts) or spared (the bile ducts, veins and gallbladder), and each one describes what the disease does there. It installs the liver and the organ viewer, and loads the liver's GLB, so it keeps that model's credit and licence. The docs list diseases in their own sidebar group.
