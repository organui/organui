Lateral views name the side they look from: the eye and prostate open "Right lateral", the nose and thymus "Left lateral", and the kidneys' "Patient left" and "Patient right" become "Left lateral" and "Right lateral", matching the other organs. Conditions that open those views use the new names. Kidney structures describe the anatomy rather than the source mesh, and the thyroid isthmus now sits over the second to fourth tracheal rings.
