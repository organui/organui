`ischaemic-stroke` shows a left middle cerebral artery stroke on the brain, opening on its left side: the frontal, parietal and temporal lobes it supplies, with the weakness, aphasia and field loss each explains. Its stages run from the hyperacute treatment window through swelling of the ventricle to a healed cavity, with the NIHSS, scans and ECG at each.
