`oesophageal-cancer` shows adenocarcinoma of the lower oesophagus on the oesophagus: Barrett's oesophagus, an early cancer cut out through the endoscope, or, as the alternative course, a locally advanced cancer found when swallowing becomes difficult. Surveillance, endoscopic resection, chemotherapy and oesophagectomy sit on its timeline.
