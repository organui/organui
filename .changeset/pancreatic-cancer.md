`pancreatic-cancer` shows an adenocarcinoma of the head of the pancreas: the blocked ducts behind painless jaundice, and the superior mesenteric artery and veins whose involvement decides whether it can be removed. Its course runs from a resectable tumour through locally advanced to metastatic disease, with the Whipple operation, chemotherapy and palliative care on the timeline.
