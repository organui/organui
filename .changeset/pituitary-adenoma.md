`pituitary-adenoma` shows a non-functioning adenoma on the pituitary and its surroundings: incidental while small, then a macroadenoma pressing on the optic chiasm (bitemporal hemianopia) and the stalk (raised prolactin), and apoplexy reaching the cavernous sinus. Each stage has the visual fields, MRI and pituitary hormones to expect.
