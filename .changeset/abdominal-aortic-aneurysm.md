`abdominal-aortic-aneurysm` shows an infrarenal aneurysm on the blood vessels, with the renal, iliac and inferior mesenteric arteries that shape its repair. Its course runs from a small aneurysm under surveillance to one large enough to repair, or rupture, with screening, surveillance, open or endovascular repair and emergency repair on the timeline.
