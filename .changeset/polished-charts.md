Charts read like shadcn's: each card ends with a headline and a muted line of context, with a trend icon where the data has a trend. Three interactive charts open the area, bar and line pages: ED arrivals with a time range, tests ordered, and cardiac rehab. Nine tooltip charts show each way to configure shadcn's chart tooltip, on a week of basal and bolus insulin doses.

Patient charts read like a monitor: heart rate, oxygen, glucose, HbA1c, blood pressure and insulin show the latest value and unit in the header, with a status badge, and heart rate and glucose shade their reference range, HbA1c and blood pressure draw their targets, census draws its staffed beds, and ED visits draws the weekly average. These charts now install shadcn's badge.
