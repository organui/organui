`crohns-disease` shows ileocolonic Crohn's disease on the intestines: transmural inflammation of the terminal ileum with skip lesions in the right colon and a spared rectum. Its course branches from a flare into a fibrotic stricture or a fistula with an abscess, with colonoscopy, induction and maintenance treatment, resection and drainage on the timeline.
