`lobar-pneumonia` shows pneumonia of the right lower lobe on the lungs. Its four segments consolidate through congestion, red and grey hepatisation and resolution; the spared middle lobe explains the silhouette sign and the open airways the air bronchograms. Each stage has the observations, bloods and chest X-ray to expect, and the CURB-65 score.
