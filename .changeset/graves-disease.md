`graves-disease` shows Graves' disease on two organs: the thyroid, with a diffuse goitre and increased blood flow, and the eye, where the extraocular muscles swell in thyroid eye disease. Its stages run from thyrotoxicosis through active eye disease to burnt-out fibrosis, with thyroid function, TSH receptor antibodies, the clinical activity score and exophthalmometry at each.
