# liver-lobule.glb attribution

`liver-lobule.glb` is an original, schematic model of a classic liver lobule: the central vein, hepatocyte plates in the three zones of the acinus, sinusoids with Kupffer cells, and a portal tract at each corner. It is generated by [`packages/registry/scripts/models/liver-lobule.ts`](../../../../packages/registry/scripts/models/liver-lobule.ts) and contains no scanned, traced or third-party data. Plates are straight and fewer than in tissue, and the portal tracts are enlarged so the triad reads; nothing is measured from micrographs.

The model is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is appreciated but not required:

> OrganUI liver lobule schematic model, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

The generator script itself is OrganUI code under this repository's MIT license. Rebuild the model with `bun scripts/models/liver-lobule.ts` from `packages/registry` rather than editing the GLB; the output is deterministic.
