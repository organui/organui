# heart.bin attribution

`heart.bin` is a point cloud sampled from the surface of the heart model that the `@organui/heart` item loads (`organui/heart-3d` at `c72ce66`, `public/models/heart.glb`), built by `apps/web/scripts/models/home-heart.ts`.

Source: the [official BodyParts3D archive](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html), BodyParts3D 4.0. See that repository's `public/models/ATTRIBUTION.md` for the full provenance.

Changes by OrganUI: on top of heart-3d's changes, the surfaces were sampled to 18,000 points, recentred and scaled to fit -1..1, and quantised to 16 bits. DBCLS does not endorse this site.
