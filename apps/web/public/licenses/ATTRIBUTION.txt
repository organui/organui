OrganUI homepage asset attribution
Recorded 2026-09-23

All preview images are actual screenshots of the working OrganUI explorers.
No anatomical content was generated, repainted, or modified for this site.
The *-cover.webp images crop the model area from the desktop screenshots,
proportionally fit it to 720 x 720 pixels using original canvas-color padding, and use WebP quality 88. Other WebP previews
compress the complete original screenshots at quality 88 without cropping.
Model colors and shapes are unchanged. CSS scales the crops proportionally.
