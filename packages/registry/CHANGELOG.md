- 3de1053: `abdominal-aortic-aneurysm` shows an infrarenal aneurysm on the blood vessels, with the renal, iliac and inferior mesenteric arteries that shape its repair. Its course runs from a small aneurysm under surveillance to one large enough to repair, or rupture, with screening, surveillance, open or endovascular repair and emergency repair on the timeline.
- 89d33bf: `acl-rupture` shows the unhappy triad on the knees of the whole-body joints model: the torn anterior cruciate ligament, medial collateral ligament and medial meniscus, with the haemarthrosis that follows. Its stages run from the acute injury to reconstruction with a graft, or to an untreated, unstable knee heading for osteoarthritis, with the Lachman and pivot shift tests and imaging at each. The rest of the body's joints fold away in the panel.
- 8cf84c9: `acute-angle-closure-glaucoma` shows the emergency on the eye: the forward lens and iris closing the drainage angle, the shallow anterior chamber and the waterlogged cornea, with the optic nerve under threat. Its stages run from the attack to laser iridotomy, or to a cupped, damaged nerve when treatment comes late, with eye pressure, acuity, gonioscopy and the visual field at each.
- f73f4f2: `acute-appendicitis` shows appendicitis on the appendix and its neighbours: early obstruction, suppuration with the psoas sign, and perforation inflaming the caecum, ileum and psoas. Each stage has the bloods, Alvarado score and imaging to expect, and the spared structures note the conditions that mimic it.
- 9190a6b: `acute-otitis-media` shows a middle ear infection on the ear: a bulging eardrum behind which the blocked auditory tube traps pus, then glue ear or, as the alternative, a perforated drum. Pain relief, antibiotics when needed, watchful waiting and grommets sit on its timeline.
- 08df52a: `addisons-disease` shows autoimmune Addison's disease on the adrenals: both cortices destroyed and the kidneys losing salt for want of aldosterone. Its course runs from gradual failure to lifelong replacement or, as the alternative, an adrenal crisis, with the Synacthen test, sick day rules and emergency hydrocortisone on the timeline.
- 32be25c: `allergy-list` shows a patient's allergies and intolerances with reaction, severity and verification, resolved ones struck through, and reads differently when allergies were not recorded and when none are known. `allergySummary` turns it into the patient banner's allergies.
- dce7166: Diseases, a new family of components on the organ viewer. `condition-viewer` shows a condition as findings on organ models: the structures it affects or spares at each stage and scale, beside a panel with its ICD-10 code, course, findings and key investigations. Affected structures are amber and spared ones muted, so a condition of a whole organ still shows. Selecting a structure in 3D or in the panel shows what the disease does there.

  `autoimmune-hepatitis` is the first: on the whole liver and on a liver lobule, through active disease, fibrosis and cirrhosis. `liver-lobule` is a new component from an original CC0 schematic model: the central vein, hepatocyte plates in their three zones, the limiting plate, sinusoids with Kupffer cells, and a portal tract at each corner.

  The organ viewer repaints when its model's colours change. In the docs, the organ viewer, anatomy tree and condition viewer form a Building blocks group, with demos that show what they do for any model. Conditions have a gallery filtered by body system, linked from the header and the sidebar.

- 71c5b69: `benign-prostatic-hyperplasia` shows an enlarging prostate on two models: the prostate, where it narrows the urethra, and the bladder, which thickens to push past it. Its stages run from troublesome symptoms to chronic retention backing up into both ureters, or acute retention, with the IPSS, flow rate, residual volume, PSA and creatinine at each.
- 368d2cf: `breast-cancer` shows a cancer of the left breast's upper outer quadrant on the breasts: ductal carcinoma in situ, an early invasive cancer, and locally advanced disease that dimples the skin through the suspensory ligaments and retracts the nipple. Screening, triple assessment, surgery, endocrine therapy and neoadjuvant treatment sit on its timeline.
- 5b37022: Two more charts in clinical forms: a NEWS2 early warning score, scored from raw observations, as a radar of each parameter's points with the risk level, and medication adherence as the proportion of days covered against the 80% threshold. The heart-rate chart marks events on its timeline. Vaccine coverage now draws each ring on a 0–100% scale, where before the highest value filled its ring, and marks the 95% target.

  Six charts are redrawn in clinical forms under the same names: admissions against the range of the past three years, the outpatient did-not-attend rate against a target, ED visits seen within four hours against a target, blood donations against the population's ABO mix as two rings, length of stay for the top diagnoses against a benchmark as a dumbbell chart, and a month of doses as a ring, each dose on time, late or missed.

- 21439ee: More components are built from shadcn's parts. - d35a5dc: `crohns-disease` shows ileocolonic Crohn's disease on the intestines: transmural inflammation of the terminal ileum with skip lesions in the right colon and a spared rectum. - 32be25c: `dashboard-02` is one patient's record on a ward: the patient banner, NEWS2 over time, the latest labs, and allergies and problems, every date in the banner's unambiguous form, with a fictional patient to replace with your records, such as from the `fhir` helper.

### Patch Changes

- d634246: ACL rupture has a timeline: the acute injury in its first weeks, reconstruction over the following year with return to sport 9 months after surgery, and, as an alternative course, rehabilitation without reconstruction. Its wording follows current guidance: many knees do well with rehabilitation alone, the graft can be quadriceps tendon, and a locked knee needs urgent surgery.
- 1119769: Organ viewers show the model credit behind a small info button in the corner, instead of a line of text under the model. The credit and licence link open in a popover. The organ viewer now installs shadcn's popover.

- a5c28ff: The dashboard-01 block's page provides its `<main>` landmark, as the explorer blocks' pages do, so `PatientDashboard` also fits inside an app layout that already has one.
- f18d89b: A clinical review brings the fifteen conditions in line with current guidelines. Ischaemic stroke starts its DOAC within days, not months, and its aphasia is global; pituitary apoplexy puts the abducens nerve inside the cavernous sinus, not in its wall; an ectopic pregnancy is diagnosed by an adnexal mass on the scan, with a pregnancy of unknown location when nothing is seen. - d5091dc: The condition viewer's scale switch draws the unselected scales in the same text colour as shadcn's tabs, which meets the WCAG AA contrast ratio on its muted background.
- be8bffa: The disease timeline's stages are one tab stop: the arrow keys, Home and End move along the course.

## 0.5.0

### Minor Changes

- 7461b36: Every item installs on shadcn's Radix styles as well as Base UI: a `{style}`
  registry URL (`https://organui.com/r/styles/{style}/{name}.json`) serves
  Radix projects a Radix build, with Radix copies of the anatomy tree, the
  organ, molecule and image viewers, the DNA helix and the blocks' components.

  New items:

  - AI Elements, built on shadcn's components: AI disclaimer, clinical
    citation (Hover Card and Button), clinical sources (Trace), differential
    list and clinician review (Item), and AI note.
  - Helpers: age, z-scores, reference ranges, clinical units and body
    measurements. The growth chart and lab results table now install the
    ones they use.
  - Utilities: one item adds the heartbeat, ECG grid and severity classes to
    the CSS file, as shadcn's utilities come together.

  Blocks: Dashboard 01, one patient at a glance with vital signs, a live ECG,
  lab results and the day's medicines.

  Charts: 21 charts of health data (area, bar, line, pie, radar and radial)
  built on shadcn's chart and card, each installable like shadcn's charts.

  Every component documents each prop with a doc comment, which the docs
  show as its API Reference.

  Blocks carry the height /blocks frames them at, in `meta.iframeHeight`.
